Appendix IV Existing instruments, work items and the standardization landscape
==============================================================================


International standards organisations address behavioural guardrails for agentic AI through a distributed portfolio spanning multiple bodies. Work clusters predominantly around AI risk management, information security, and identity frameworks, with sector-specific safety standards contributing functional and automotive perspectives. Agent-specific behavioural guardrail specification remains an emerging area, with several work items under development in ITU-T and related forums.

Existing instruments and work items
-----------------------------------


ISO and IEC
~~~~~~~~~~~


ISO/IEC 22989 — Artificial intelligence — Concepts and terminology. Establishes terminology for artificial intelligence and describes concepts in the field of AI. Provides a common vocabulary for the development of other standards and supports communication among diverse stakeholders.

ISO/IEC 23894 — Artificial intelligence — Guidance on risk management. Provides guidance on how organizations that develop, produce, deploy or use AI products, systems and services can manage risk specifically related to AI. Describes processes for effective implementation and integration of AI risk management.

ISO/IEC 42001 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system. Specifies requirements and provides guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an AI management system within the context of an organization. Intended for organizations providing or using products or services that utilize AI systems.

ISO/IEC 27001 — Information security management systems. Specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving an information security management system within the context of the organization. Includes requirements for the assessment and treatment of information security risks tailored to organizational needs.

ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 — Artificial intelligence. Develops horizontal standards for AI, including work on risk management, management systems, governance implications and conformity assessment. Maintains numerous liaisons with other ISO and IEC committees and joint working groups on topics such as functional safety for AI.

ISO 26262 — Road vehicles — Functional safety. Addresses possible hazards caused by malfunctioning behaviour of safety-related electrical and/or electronic systems in series production road vehicles. Provides a framework for functional safety to assist the development of safety-related systems at the system level.

ISO 21434 — Road vehicles — Cybersecurity engineering. Specifies engineering requirements for cybersecurity risk management regarding concept, product development, production, operation, maintenance and decommissioning of electrical and electronic systems in road vehicles, including components and interfaces.

ISO 10218 — Robots and robotic devices — Safety requirements. Specifies requirements for the inherently safe design, risk reduction measures and information for use of robots for an industrial environment. Addresses the robot as an incomplete machine.

ISO/TS 15066 — Collaborative robots. Addresses safety requirements for collaborative industrial robot systems and the work environment, supplementing ISO 10218. Provides guidance on collaborative operation where humans and robots share a workspace.

IEC 61508 — Functional safety of electrical/electronic/programmable electronic safety-related systems. Contains requirements to minimize dangerous failures in E/E/PE safety-related systems. Applies irrespective of application and provides a risk-based process for determining required performance of safety-related systems.

IEC 61511 — Functional safety — Safety instrumented systems for the process industry sector. Gives requirements for the specification, design, installation, operation and maintenance of safety instrumented systems for the process industry. Developed as a process sector implementation of IEC 61508.

IEC 62443 — Security for industrial automation and control systems. Addresses security for industrial automation and control systems through a series of standards defining requirements and guidance for secure development, integration and operation of IACS.

ITU-T
~~~~~


XSTR.magAI-behavior — Security framework for emergent and misaligned AI behaviour. Describes a security framework for emergent, misaligned and multi-objective behaviours in advanced AI systems. Establishes taxonomy, control plane, drift-measurement procedure and detection capability for adversarial exploitation of structural bias in collective choice operators.

XSTR.ltf-AAI — Trust framework landscape for agentic AI. Provides an overview of trust, risk and security considerations relevant to agentic AI systems. Identifies challenges, reviews existing mechanisms and proposes conceptual directions for a unified framework supporting verifiable accountability and adaptive trust assessment.

XSTR.sem-AIA — Security evaluation methods for AI agents. Focuses on providing methods guidance for assessing the security of AI agents. Defines security evaluation indicators for core agent capabilities including perception, cognition, planning, memory and action, and provides recommended testing methods.

XSTR.atcp — Agent trust confidence primitives. Specifies cryptographic primitives for quantification and verifiable communication of trust confidence between AI agents. Defines trust score structures, confidence interval representations, trust assertion atoms and trust composition procedures for multi-agent scenarios.

XSTR.aam — AI accountability manifest format. Specifies cryptographic accountability mechanisms for AI agents through verifiable manifest declarations. Defines manifest format specifications binding agent identity to declared intent and operational scope, intent verification procedures and accountability chain integrity mechanisms.

XSTR.AI-GSB — AI governance and security baseline. Provides guidelines for security benchmarks for foundation models. Contains baseline text for assessing security characteristics of foundation models.

X.aaia-sec — Security framework for autonomous AI agents. Proposes a comprehensive security framework for autonomous AI agents covering initial phase security, runtime phase security and security observation. Addresses control plane risks, skills supply chain risks, memory risks and high-privilege runtime execution risks.

X.S-AIAI — Security framework and requirements for invocation by AI agents. Identifies threats to invocation by AI agents of other agents, external tools and modular capability units. Proposes a security framework for invocation workflows addressing data leakage, protocol poisoning, tool misuse and identity impersonation.

X.sr-taimas — Security requirements for terminal-based AI multi-agent systems. Describes security threats to terminal-based AI multi-agent systems and provides security architecture and requirements. Addresses agent identity security, data protection during transmission, collaborative communication and task coordination risks.

X.sg-AIAof — Security guidelines for cloud-side AI agent orchestration frameworks. Provides security guidelines for AI agent orchestration frameworks deployed on the cloud side.

X.1254 — Entity authentication assurance framework. Defines four levels of entity authentication assurance and criteria for each level. Provides guidance on mapping other assurance frameworks, exchanging authentication results and control methods for mitigating authentication threats.

FG-TIDA — Focus Group on Trust and Identity for Humans and Agentic AI. Addresses trust management and interoperable digital identity infrastructure for humans and agentic AI. Supports development of secure, trustworthy digital ecosystems where humans and agentic AI interact and collaborate.

IEEE
~~~~


IEEE 7009-2024 — Fail-safe design for autonomous and semi-autonomous systems. Addresses fail-safe design principles for autonomous and semi-autonomous systems.

NIST
~~~~


NIST AI RMF 1.0 — AI Risk Management Framework. Provides a framework for managing risks associated with AI systems. Organizes risk management activities across govern, map, measure and manage functions.

NIST AI 600-1 — Generative AI Profile. Provides a profile of risks specific to generative AI systems. Supplements the AI Risk Management Framework with considerations for generative AI.

NIST CAISI — Centre for AI Standards and Innovation work on evaluation and red-teaming. Conducts work on evaluation and red-teaming methodologies for AI systems. Supports development of standards and practices for AI testing and assessment.

ETSI
~~~~


ISG SAI / TC SAI reports — AI threat ontology, security testing, securing AI and mitigating threats from AI. Produces reports addressing AI threat ontology, security testing methodologies, securing AI systems and mitigating threats from AI.

TS 104 050 — Cybersecurity for artificial intelligence. Addresses cybersecurity aspects specific to artificial intelligence systems.

Others
~~~~~~


IETF RFC 6749 — OAuth 2.0 Authorization Framework. Specifies the OAuth 2.0 authorization framework enabling third-party applications to obtain limited access to an HTTP service.

IETF RFC 9334 — Remote ATtestation procedureS (RATS) architecture. Defines an architecture for remote attestation procedures, including roles, terminology and information flow for attestation of system integrity.

IETF RFC 9711 — Protocol for delegating credentials / agent authorization. Specifies a protocol for delegating credentials and agent authorization.

IETF draft — AI-agent object capability model. Proposes an object capability model for AI agents.

OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. Identifies the top ten security risks for applications built using large language models.

OWASP Agentic AI security guidance. Provides security guidance for agentic AI systems.

CSA AI Controls Matrix (AICM). Provides a controls matrix for AI systems addressing security, privacy and compliance considerations.
